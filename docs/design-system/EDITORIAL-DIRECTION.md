# Editorial — design direction

## Intent

Editorial is Design 03: a contemporary independent publication for cultural, architectural and art-directed digital work. Typography creates the architecture; paper tone, rules and measured whitespace create the pacing.

## Foundations

- Canvas: warm paper `#F2EEE4`
- Primary surface: paper shadow `#E9E2D5`
- Secondary surface: deeper stock `#DED5C6`
- Ink: near-black `#1D1B18`
- Muted text: `#746E65`
- Accent and focus: oxblood `#9D3C2F`
- Rules: `#C5BBAC`

Raised and recessed meaning is expressed through stock changes, rules and composition rather than shadows. Functional status colours remain reserved for later feedback work.

## Typography

The display voice uses the available Georgia serif stack, with Arial/Helvetica as the precise reading and metadata sans. The deliberate contrast is more important than a downloaded typeface at this phase: oversized serif headlines set gesture, while compact sans labels hold navigation and evidence.

Display headings use tight tracking and short intentional line breaks. Reading copy uses a restrained measure and generous line-height. Captions, figure labels and numerical notation stay compact but never below legible mobile sizes.

## Geometry

Editorial does not alter the shared workspace container, sidebar, stage or breakpoints. Within the stage, it uses fine horizontal rules, asymmetric columns, numbered details and paper-tone changes. Grids collapse to one column before text becomes clipped.

## Interaction

Future interactions should be precise: underline movement, image framing, restrained reveal and page-like disclosures. No luminous, glass or physical-depth effects are part of the Editorial recipe. Reduced motion removes nonessential transitions.

## Phase status

Implemented: Foundations, Typography, Actions, Forms, Disclosure, Feedback, Data Display, Carousels, Overlays and Advanced, each with shared comparison references and editorial signature studies.

Actions use rules, ink fills, numbering and directional treatment rather than physical or luminous depth. Forms use publication labels and underlined fields. Disclosure uses numbered sections and typographic separators. Feedback uses annotations, proof-status language and restrained ink/accent signals. Data uses a feature index, figures and rule-led evidence rather than dashboard panels. Carousels move as deliberate magazine sequences. Overlays are paper sheets, and Advanced controls are precise editorial instruments.

Pending: Motion and Effects. Their current Editorial states intentionally reserve the shared Lab position without pretending implementation exists.
