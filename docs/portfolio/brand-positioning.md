# LAC STUDIO — Brand positioning

Status: Phase 1 strategy; no production copy has been changed.

## Primary positioning

**From idea to working product.**

LAC STUDIO is an independent software development studio led by Andrei Lucaciu. It helps organisations and founders turn a business problem, service idea or manual workflow into a working digital product: web application, Android workflow, SaaS platform, internal system or customer-facing website.

The line should remain the primary positioning. It is short, understandable and supported by the public work. The supporting proposition needs to carry the specificity:

> I design and build the interface, backend, data model, integrations and release path as one connected product.

This is more commercial than “creative development” alone and more honest than positioning LAC STUDIO as a large agency.

## Core value proposition

Clients work directly with the person responsible for shaping and building the product. This keeps the problem, interface, technical architecture and delivery connected instead of distributing responsibility across agency handoffs.

LAC STUDIO is best positioned for work where the product has to do practical work: coordinate people, expose operational status, support a service journey, work in uncertain connectivity, or replace fragmented tools.

## Priority audiences

1. **Small and medium-sized organisations with an operational problem.** Strongest fit and strongest evidence: clinic, laboratory, restaurant and field-work products. They need a system, not merely a marketing site.
2. **Founders and professional service businesses starting a focused product.** Relevant where a founder needs one technical partner to shape an MVP, customer portal or SaaS foundation.
3. **Businesses replacing spreadsheets, paper, disconnected tools or manual status updates.** A highly understandable problem category that maps to Dental Lab Management, GR Plus, MedicHelp and Restaurant SaaS.
4. **Teams with field, Android or offline needs.** A narrower but distinctive technical niche demonstrated by GR Plus and the unpublished Android work.
5. **Agencies needing an independent technical collaborator.** Valid secondary audience, but should not dominate the first version of the site until examples of that collaboration are public.

Hospitality, restaurant and e-commerce websites demonstrate range, but should support—not dilute—the primary position around complex, operational software.

## Differentiators

- One accountable person across product direction, interface and engineering.
- Demonstrated work in operational environments: healthcare, dental laboratories, restaurants and field teams.
- Capability from customer-facing experience through backend, database, roles, integrations and release.
- Offline-first and role-based workflow experience.
- A carefully designed visual point of view, demonstrated by the five shared design systems.
- Psychology-informed observation of people, friction and decision-making.

The last point is a differentiator, not a substitute for evidence of engineering competence.

## Psychology + engineering narrative

Use this relationship as a single idea:

> Understanding people makes better software possible. Engineering makes that understanding useful.

Psychology gives Andrei a lens for observing behaviour, context, cognitive friction, communication and the decisions people need to make. Engineering turns that understanding into actual product behaviour: roles, flows, interface states, data and integrations.

Appropriate places: About, a concise Home proof point, selected case studies and founder schema later. Avoid presenting psychology as formal UX research, clinical expertise, a promise of behavioural outcomes, or a reason clients should overlook technical proof.

## Messaging hierarchy

1. **What:** Complete digital products for real business work.
2. **Who it serves:** Organisations, service businesses and founders with a process, customer journey or product idea to improve.
3. **How:** Direct collaboration with one person across product thinking, UX/UI and software engineering.
4. **Proof:** Published applications and sites with clear problem, scope, platform and technical evidence.
5. **Why it feels different:** Psychology-informed attention to people plus technical ability to build the full system.
6. **Action:** Start a project conversation by email or form.

## Voice system

| Principle | Apply it like this | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear before clever | Name the type of software and business problem. | Abstract headlines that need a paragraph to decode. |
| Specific before broad | “Web applications, operational systems and offline workflows.” | “Digital experiences for tomorrow.” |
| Singular and accountable | Use “I” for founder responsibility; “LAC STUDIO” for the brand. | “We” when it implies a non-existent team. |
| Technically credible | Mention architecture only where it supports a buyer decision or case study. | Jargon lists in marketing copy. |
| Human and calm | Describe the client’s context without hype. | “World-class”, “cutting-edge”, “seamless”, “revolutionary”. |
| Premium through restraint | Use concise, precise sentences and honest scope. | Artificial urgency or inflated claims. |

## Recommended descriptions

### Short

LAC STUDIO is an independent software development studio that turns business problems and product ideas into working web, mobile and operational software.

### Long

LAC STUDIO is led by Andrei Lucaciu, an independent product developer with a background in psychology and more than seven years of professional software development experience. I design and build complete digital products—from the interface and user flow to backend services, databases, integrations and launch. The work is especially suited to organisations that need a clearer customer journey, a dependable internal system or a practical way to replace manual, disconnected processes.

## Claims to avoid

- “Full-service agency”, “team”, “award-winning” or “industry-leading” without evidence.
- Claimed revenue, users, time savings, uptime, adoption or satisfaction figures that cannot be published and verified.
- Claims of formal UX research, clinical psychology outcomes or accessibility/legal compliance unless project-specific evidence exists.
- iOS, macOS, enterprise scale, global availability or sector compliance claims without documented proof.
- “AI-powered” unless a public product and its actual AI capability are described.
